Capture my browser as a "real user" profile

Open this page in the browser you use every day (not a driven one). It records the headers and sensor signals your browser sends, then a test click, and saves them to tools/profiles/captured-<name>.json. From then on tools/legit_traffic.py replays you on every checkpoint, so you will see straight away if a defense you write blocks your own browser.

Nothing leaves your machine: the store runs on localhost.
