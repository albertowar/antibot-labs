# Capstone brief: Govern the Agents

**Scenario.** It is launch day for the *Lab Runner 9* drop on the Lab Store. For
three days you built the AI-era stack piece by piece. Today you run all of it at
once, against everything that shows up, under the AI governance record your team
wrote in Workshop 4 Chapter 8.

**Who must get in.**
- Human shoppers in six cohorts: desktop, an office NAT and an RPA macro behind one
  IP, a VPN exit, privacy browsers, screen-reader and voice-control users, and
  shoppers driving their own local agent.
- A signed cloud agent from a trusted operator, acting on a mandate from the shopper.
- A verified search crawler (indexing is revenue) and a paying AI-training crawler.

**Who must not — or must pay.**
- A training crawler that ignores the reservation, a Googlebot-style impostor, a
  stealth crawler from a cloud box.
- An undeclared agent farm, a cloud agent that stops declaring when punished, a
  forged `Signature-Agent`, an assistive-technology mimic.
- An LLM extractor reading the catalogue, and an injection campaign against the
  store's own assistant.

**The rules you run under (your W4 Ch8 governance record).**
- Decoys go only to traffic you would otherwise refuse, never to a crawler that
  honoured the reservation and never to a session using the accessibility API.
- Every challenge has a non-visual path. Every block links an appeal a person reviews.
- The assistant says it is an AI (EU AI Act Article 50). A human approves every new rule.
- Logs are kept 90 days and no longer, and the store can prove it.

**How you win.** The scoreboard has three columns: bots through, legitimate users
**blocked**, and legitimate users **challenged** (weighted by whether the challenge
has a non-visual path and whether the person behind an agent ever sees it). A block
of a real user is a failure. A challenge is a cost you count, not a failure.

Bring your governance record from Workshop 4 Chapter 8 (or use
`solutions/round4/governance_record.md`). The team that found the most holes in
Chapter 8 audits in Round 4.
